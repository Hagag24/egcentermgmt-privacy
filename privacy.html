Legal & privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 19, 2026
This Privacy Policy explains how EgCenterMgmt handles information when it is used by authorized organizations and users. The exact information processed depends on how each organization configures and uses the application.

1. Information processed by the application

EgCenterMgmt may process information entered by authorized users as part of normal business and administrative operations. Depending on configuration, this may include names and account information, contact information, student or customer records, employee or staff records, attendance and operational records, financial or payment-related administrative records, and other information entered by authorized users while using the application.

2. How information is used

Information is used only to provide the application's business and administrative functions, including managing records, users, attendance, operations, payments, inventory, reports and related services. EgCenterMgmt does not use personal information for advertising purposes.

3. Data storage and control

Application data may be stored in databases and storage systems configured by the organization using the application. Data retention is determined by the organization's operational requirements and applicable legal obligations. Organizations remain responsible for their own access policies, retention choices and lawful use of information entered into the system.

4. Data sharing

EgCenterMgmt does not sell personal information. Information may be accessible to authorized users of the organization and may be shared with service providers or connected systems only when required to operate the application or provide requested services.

5. Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure or loss. Access to application functions and information is limited according to user accounts, permissions and roles configured by the organization.

6. Internet access

The application may use an internet connection to communicate with authorized backend services, APIs, authentication services, application services, licensing or Microsoft Store services required for installation, operation or updates.

7. User rights and data requests

Users who wish to access, correct, update or request deletion of personal information associated with their use of the application should contact the organization that operates their deployment or contact application support. Requests are handled in accordance with applicable laws and organizational policies.

8. Children's information

EgCenterMgmt is an administrative and management system. Information relating to minors must be entered and managed only by authorized organizations and users in accordance with applicable laws, parental or guardian requirements where applicable, and organizational policies.

9. Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application functionality, data practices or legal requirements change. The latest version will remain available on this page.

10. Contact

For privacy questions, support requests or data-related inquiries, contact mohamed.dev.eg@gmail.com.
